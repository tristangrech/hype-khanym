$HYPE v2 trades on uselaunch.fun, priced in real $HYPE (Hyperliquid's token, bridged to Solana by Wormhole). Every trade pays uselaunch a 3% fee, split three ways.

  1. 1% buys back $HYPE (ours) and burns it.
  2. 1% buys and burns $LAUNCH.

Nobody gets paid for holding. Burns depend on trading volume: no volume, no burns. The price is quoted in real HYPE, so it also moves with Hyperliquid's price. uselaunch runs the fees and burns, not us.
